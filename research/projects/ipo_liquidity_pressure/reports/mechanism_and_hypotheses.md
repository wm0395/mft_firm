# IPO Mechanism And Hypotheses

This report states the core research mechanism in a reviewable form.

## Mechanism

1. IPO attention rises around rumor, DRHP, and price-band discovery.
2. Investors prepare capital for applications and allotment.
3. Deployable liquidity in secondary-market baskets weakens because cash is
   reserved, blocked, or pre-positioned.
4. After allotment, unused funds are released and may return to secondary
   market baskets or the new listing.

## Hypotheses

- Hypothesis 0: large IPOs create measurable temporary liquidity blocking.
- Hypothesis 1: vulnerable baskets underperform before or during the
  application window.
- Hypothesis 2: the blocking window suppresses buying rather than always
  creating obvious selling.
- Hypothesis 3: underperformance mean-reverts after allotment or unblocking.
- Hypothesis 4: pull and release are linked mechanically but may hit different
  baskets.

## Observable Evidence

- IPO size relative to rolling market turnover.
- Subscription intensity and category mix.
- Secondary-market turnover, breadth, and buying intensity.
- Windowed abnormal returns around the application and release phases.

## Failure Modes

- ASBA blocking is too small relative to market liquidity.
- The effect is attention-driven rather than liquidity-driven.
- Fund release rotates into the new listing instead of secondary baskets.
- Market regime dominates the windowed return signal.
- The observed move is fundamental, not temporary.

## Research Stance

This report does not claim a tradeable edge. It only defines the mechanism and
the hypotheses that the data must either support or reject.
