# Review Packs

This directory is for compact human review records promoted from lab reports.

A review pack should include:

- the exact universe configuration,
- the selected alpha and expression mode set,
- backtest and cost assumptions,
- chart inspection notes,
- selector precision, coverage, abstention, and baseline lift,
- robustness results from holdout, walk-forward, purged, and embargo tests.

Review packs are evidence summaries only. They are not deployment approvals.
